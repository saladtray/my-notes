
## Democratic Breakdown 
- People support regimes for different reasons
- Normative sources: Regime is congruent with their values
- Instrumental Sources: people support the regimes because it provides good outcomes
- Democratic breakdown is multidimensional

### Calls for Democracy 
"All efforts to combat poverty will fail unless they take place under a democratic framework that gives the means to obtain water, bread, and education." - EZLN, Mexican Guerilla 

### Calls for Strongmen?
- When governments systematically fail to protect their people there be an open door for backsliding 

### Public Commitment to Democracy 
- presidential approval seems connected to regime approval

### Impact of Drug War
- The instrumental basis of democratic support makes the inability to provide security a threat of the regime
- Insecurity affects everyone, but low-income people suffer more 
- Experience with democracy seems to help

### Leadership Erosion Theory
- Democracies **Break down when**
	- leaders give a voice and power to extremists
	- Politicians use every tool to depower and delegitimize their opponents
	- Partisan polarization increases as heterogenous interests take control of different parties 
#### But also Consider
- Definition of extremist is tricky. Based on whatever the status quo is 
- Ideally, we want our systems to be robust individual decisions
- Inclusion is the defining characteristic of our democracy

# What is Populism Anyways?
- populists are often blamed for democratic backsliding
- Pure people vs the corrupted elite

### Incomplete Transitions Theory
- 66% of new democracies inherited constitutions from authoritarian predecessors
- These democracies free people from repression but don't give equal weight in deciding policy
- Tutelary Democracy: issues protecting the ousted regime are not subject to democratic contestation
- This makes anti-systemic messaging (or populist ideas) appealing to the masses

### Tutelary Democracy in Mexico
- After 2000 PRI fell but not the organizations that sustained it
- Former allies of the PRI kept their clout and used it gain influence in the democratic government
- Democratic governments were unable to legislate on the issues that affected these groups 

## Paths to Democratic Erosion
- Leadership erosion that opens the door for authoritarians
- Incomplete transitions to democracy that breed social discontent
