## Political Regimes
- Remember our definition of regimes: The norms and institutions that regulate the competition for power
- There an infinite number of potential regimes, some democratic and some autocratic
- We will focus on two broad types and two questions
	- What makes an autocracy become a democracy
	- What makes democracies become autocracies?

### Resilience of Political Regimes
- Most regimes collapse soon after emerging. Only a few last 
- But once established, they tend to last for a long time 
- Powerful people want to remain powerful. How do weaker groups defeat them? 
- Incumbents not committed to the transition of power act proactively
- For example, repression increases on the months heading to contested elections 

## Reasons for Democratization
1. International agents and dynamics weaken foreign support for dictatorships, and this leads to democratization at home. 
2. Economic circumstances lead to class struggle that results in democratization
3. Cultural Change makes autocracies unpopular and pressures for democratization emerge
