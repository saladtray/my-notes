- Very large country
- Urbanized in some areas but not so much in other areas. Well developed but very unequal
- Presidential democracy elected every 6 years
- traditionally, a three-party system

## Prehispanic Civilizations
Warring different cultures. Biggest empire was the Aztecs, they were centralized. Very religious society. 

## New Spain - Mexico during the Colonial Period
- Mexico was a Spanish colony from 1521 to 1810
- Became Catholic during this time
	- Church was the main political authority during this time
- Period marked by exploitation and *mestizaje*
	- Racial mixing between Spaniards and indigenous populations 
- Operated under de-centralized authorities - Spanish not able to keep track of such a large territory
## Independence (1810)
- France defeats Spain in a war, Mexican elite rally and fight off Spanish
**Mexico after independence**
- Characterized by anarchy
- Made worse by the incursions by the French and Spanish
- Country was bankrupt and scarcely populated. Started to breakdown
- Led to Mexican-American War (1846-1848)
- No common identity among Mexicans 
- Didn't believe in the legitimacy of the central government 

## Mexico after the Mexican-American War
- Mexico under **Benito Juarez**, rebuilds by stopping foreign payments and creating a more powerful government 
- Internal turmoil because of political changes, leading to civil war (1857)
- France invades Mexico forming a short lived 'empire' (1864)
- Juarez loses power during this conflict
- **Porfirio Diaz** ascends to power and rules for 31 years

## The Mexican Revolution
- Generals rebel against dictator in 1910
- Continue agenda of creating a strong government
- form the PRI to incorporate all dissenting forces

Biggest issue that Mexico has - the weakness of the state.
