Comparative politics systematic search for answers to political questions about how people around the world make and contest authoritative public choices. 
	The scientific study of politics in different contexts

Science has two elements
1. Science must be theoretical: collection of related statements logically connected to each other
	1. allows us to make "if then" statements (hypotheses)
	2. allows us to understand what to change to obtain the desired effect
	3. difference between alchemy and chemistry 
2. Science must give ==falsifiable== predictions
	1. theories can be proven wrong
	2. "all democracies eventually collapse" - cannot be proven wrong and thus it is unscientific 
	3. in contrast "no democracy with GDP per capita above 650 will collapse"

Prediction vs Science

Democratic Peace Result: Looking at history. If we know a country is democratic, then they will not go to war with each other.

Democratic Durability Result: Throughout history, there has never been no democracy above 650 GDP per capita that have turned autocratic. 

Although these statements are accurate but they don't explain the world. *They are atheoretical*
