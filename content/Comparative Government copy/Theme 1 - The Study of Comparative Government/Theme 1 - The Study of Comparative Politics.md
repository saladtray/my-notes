**Politics as Relationships**

If politics is not about government or cooperation, then what is it? 
	"Politics is the activity of aggregating and defending our friends, and dispersing and fighting our enemies" - Norberto Bobbio

Carl Schmitt (Nazi Party Member) defined politics as friend-enemy relationships
- enemies are those who will confront you and will fight you back
- not necessarily about war
	- Fred Hampton "what is war...what is politics?"
**Politics is about confrontation among opposing groups**

"Every religious, moral, economic, or ethical antithesis transforms into a political one if it sufficiently strong to group human beings effectively according to friend and enemy. " - Schmitt

**Cooperation in enemy-friend relationships**
- existential risk is ineradicable from political 
- but the political also unites for the designation of an enemy creates communities of friends
- enemies as *pharmakon*

"...Racial discrimination furnished grist for the Communist propaganda mills" Brown v Board. Amicus brief
- Friend-enemy relationships create cooperation based on self interest

**Limitations of Schmitt's definition**
- Schmitt's ideas require an intention to confront but some actions are political without intention
