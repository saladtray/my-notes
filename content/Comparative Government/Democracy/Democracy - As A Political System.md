### The Mixed Constitution Problem
How do we maintain the effective rule and order associated with dictatorship but keep the pluralism associated with democracy? 

### Hobbes - Leviathan I
- What do we need to avoid chaos? A sovereign (king)
	- An authority that in indivisible and permanent 

## Rousseau - The Sovereignty Of People
- "Men are born free, but everywhere they find themselves in chains"
- The only legitimate government is one where people retain their government
- Popular will is the sovereign not monarchs and not individuals 
	- basic level of consensus between differing groups 
- Weaknesses
	- if we try and find a common consensus and the matter is that they have conflicting views such as on citizenship. Then what does the popular consensus entail. Some people will have the political capacity to force something onto the popular will. 

**Jacobinism**: People have the authority to decide on anything because they have the sovereign. Led to democratic terror. Idea was that Rousseau's idea would make way for this to happen. 

### Liberalism: Constitution Under Sovereignty
Liberalism is characterized by
- The primacy of the individual
	- No popular will over individuals
- The primacy of human rights
	- No tyranny by the few or the many
	- Life, liberty, property 
- There is a core of inalienable principles that even the sovereign cannot challenge. 

## John Locke
- Offered a view of democracy that was based in liberalism
	- Humans have inalienable rights that no consensus can take away
- How do we protect individuals from the government
- ==Divide the government
- Separate the judiciary from parliament
- Parliament still holds exec power
- Parliament is divided between house of commons and lords

Giving authority to many groups without falling into anarchy

### Counterweights after the American Revolution
- Madison wanted to avoid mass tyranny
- His answer was checks and balances
	- Checks are not inherently good. Checks and balances can be used to undermine majority rule and maintain the status quo
- "Ambition must be made to counteract ambition"
	- Executive vs legislative vs judiciary
	- Federal vs local government 
	- Judicial review 

### Federalism
Two or more governments share (non-overlapping) authority over a portion of the territory in a country
- Unitary systems: A central government holds all authority over the entire country
How does federalism help prevent tyranny?

#### Limitations of Checks and Balances
- May be unnecessary to prevent tyranny
- Political parties
- They can make government dysfunctional, leading back to anarchy
	- Constant checking and overreach 
- There are democracies that function well without checks and balances
	- The UK operates in a stable manner without checks and balances since it has more distinct separation of power

### Democracy vs Oligarchy 
**Oligarchy**: Rule by the few 
- What does it take to have an effective organization
	- Centralized leadership
	- Bureaucrats with technical knowledge
	- Hierarchal relationships
- Things that are conducive to a strong state, are things that can also lead to an oligarchy
- These are also the same tools needed to dominate dissent
	- Control information flow
	- Reward loyalty 
##### ^ Applies to political groups as well

#### The "Iron Law of Oligarchy" in Political Parties
- Authority can only be exercised by a small group 
- Michels: "Because a minority always rules and their interests will always be different, government decisions cannot match the preferences of the true majority"
- parties have access to the state and monopolize being able to get into government
	- "Grab onto power and don't let anybody in"
	- "By the act of being in the government you develop preferences different from the preferences of the people"
- Why do some countries develop oligarchies? Why do some develop representative democracies 

### Majority Rule
- Motivated by the principle of popular sovereignty
- assumes political equality 
- The rule: "in order for x to be policy is is necessary and sufficient that those who prefer x are more numerous than those who do not"
#### Problems With Majority Rule
- What if some are indifferent between options
- What happens if there is a tie
- ==Condorcet Dilemma==

### Working Definitions of a Democracy
"A political system in which rulers are accountable to the ruled" - Samuels. 

"Democracy is a **political regime** that provides constitutional opportunities for changing government, and a **social mechanism** to allow a large portion of the population to influence policy through **elections** between competing candidates" S.M Lipset *The Political Man*

## Pluralism
- A theory about what are the social conditions that make democracy work
- Empower all sorts of political groups
	- Those who fight each other will wind up at a social consensus 
- **Participation**: civic liberties and socioeconomic opportunities. Everyone should be able to participate in politics even those outside of the ruling minority. 
- **Contestation**: Keep the political regime open. Groups should be able to shape policy through participation without the threat of violence. 
	- High number of pressure points
	- Pressure Points: opportunities to influence politics such as elections, protest, letters, local vs federal, judicial review 

#### It's All About Political Groups
- Government in inherently elitist. But people can build political groups for influence
	- Unions, social movements, ethnic groups, etc.
- These political groups can counteract the monopolizing tendencies of the government elite 
- These can be used by the same social elite
	- NRA, APAC, etc.

#### Critiques of Pluralism
- Who can leverage the power of interest groups?
- Wealth and socioeconomic advantages facilitate organizations 
- Massive organizations can outweigh smaller ones 
- Leaders have very distinct issues
	- In a women's rights group - A leader may solely look at an issue dependent on gender but may not look at how a poor woman lives, therefore the subgroup cannot influence the leadership is not poor
- The proliferation of political organizations does not guarantee that marginalized groups will get a say in politics
- Some political organization are better able to access the state than others.
