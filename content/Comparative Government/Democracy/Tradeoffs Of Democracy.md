### Core Values of Democracy: Participation
- Participation: Involvement in the last instance of the decision making (i.e. voting on legislation not for representatives)
### Core Values of Democracy: Deliberation
- Engagement with opposing views to try and reach consensus 
	- Argumentation is most effective when not self-interested 
	- Allows for more inclusive decisions 
	- Can breed toleration for opposing views

#### Participation Needs Deliberation 

#### Referenda as a method of decisions
- Can maximize participation
- Not especially deliberative
	- Options are set in stone. Negates deliberation
	- Results in zero-sum. No negotiation 

### Why Don't We Agree On Democracy
- Definitions of democracy are not neutral
- They emphasize some aspects at the expense of others
	- Participation, Equality, Deliberation
