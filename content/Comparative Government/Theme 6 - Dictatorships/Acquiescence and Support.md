
### Public Acquiescence
- **Totalitarian governments** are those that manage to impose a common ideology
- Everything is seen in function of that ideology
- Their power resides on acquiescence to the ideology 
- Examples
	- Nazi Germany, Soviet Union 
	- Nationalism can be used to build a common identity. However the Nazis used nationalism to determine how people should operate in their private life, creating new norms and going beyond politics. 
- Surveillance is part and parcel of dictatorship
- Panopticon: People obey because they feel they are being watched
- But it goes further in totalitarian governments
- "Workers of the world united!" = "I behave in the manner expected of me. I am obedient and deserve to be left in peace."
- Public acquiescence is a signal to the authority
- But also a signal to the public - that's there's only one right option
- Spiral of Silence: once people stop dissenting in public, the dominant ideology is internalized 

### Authoritarian Ideologies
- An ideology is set of political beliefs that structures political thinking and gives meaning to political life
- Communist ideology
	- Everything is seen in terms of class warfare
	- The proletariat is good and pure, the bourgeoise is evil
	- Sacrifices needed in order to defeat capitalism
